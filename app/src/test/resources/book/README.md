# Service book fixtures

Public Azbyka worship pages downloaded on 2026-11-11 for parser regression tests

Source pattern: `https://azbyka.ru/worships/?date=YYYY-MM-DD&worship=KIND&size=full&church=30`

The fixtures retain the selected date, service kind, full-size selector, church and feast selectors, title and complete service content DOM. Unrelated site navigation is omitted

- `liturgy-2026-11-11`, weekday `vespers`, `matins`, `hour1` for 2026-11-12 were the initial full-book fixtures
- `chas3`, `chas6`, `chas9` for 2026-11-12 exercise each hour's distinct closing prayer
- `liturgy`, `vespers`, `matins` for 2026-11-15 exercise Sunday orders, including Vespers ending with the blessing before Matins
- `liturgy`, `matins` for 2026-04-12 exercise Paschal orders, including Matins without the ordinary Six Psalms
- `vespers-2026-04-12` is an intentional negative fixture: the endpoint returns a Paschal Midnight Office instead of Vespers, with `midnight` as the first selector option and no selected option. It must be rejected for a Vespers request. A separate regression also forces the selector to match and checks the actual service title

Tests also cut these real DOMs at several positions and remove their last content paragraph. Presence of an earlier "Amen" must not make a truncated service pass validation

- `evening-psalm-originals.json` preserves 10 complete Church Slavonic Psalms that match the dated books after matching Psalm-number headings are separated on both sides of a source row
- `evening-prayer-originals.json` preserves a complete matching original from each of the six added evening/hour sources
- `calendar-hymns-2026-11-11.html` preserves the calendar page's canonical date, heading, paired DOM structure and eight complete Church Slavonic originals. Modern Russian translations are replaced with explicit synthetic markers; six pairs match the actual liturgy fixture. Source: `https://azbyka.ru/days/2026-11-11`

- `translation-paired-structure-full.html` and `translation-paired-structure-short.html` preserve eleven actual Church Slavonic rows and their paired DOM boundaries from the published liturgy editions. Russian prayer bodies are replaced by explicit synthetic markers; choir labels, threefold-repeat markers, matching h3 headings and alternating 1./2. edition markers remain. These markers are not biblical verse numbers. `translation-paired-provenance.json` records source URLs, row indexes and source/fixture SHA-256 hashes. The fixtures exercise thirteen repeated visible blocks in the 2026-11-12 audit; they do not ship modern translated prayers in the APK

- `liturgy-2026-11-12.html` is the dated original-language order used for the Russian-rubric audit, with site navigation removed using Jsoup. The parsed full document and cleaned fixture were verified equal before the classification change. No modern parallel translation was added
- `rubric-classification-provenance.json` identifies 27 whole Russian directions/headings (9 visible, 18 altar) in the actual 11/12/15 November documents. It records exact source URLs and a baseline hash of the complete original text in reading order. For 12 November, the full downloaded document and cleaned fixture have separate SHA-256 values; for 11/15 November, the audit inputs are the existing trimmed fixtures and no full-download hash is claimed. Directions containing quoted chant openings remain whole directions; an appended prayer cannot inherit their classification

- `translation-leading-metadata-short.html` and `translation-leading-metadata-full.html` retain fourteen actual source rows, their complete Church Slavonic originals and the separate response/prayer boundary. Modern Russian prayer bodies are replaced with explicit synthetic markers; only speaker labels and the verified leading directions remain. `translation-leading-metadata-provenance.json` records source/fixture hashes, zero-based source rows, seven dated-book paragraphs and their baseline reading-order hash. Five paragraphs were formerly false RUBRIC; the weekday entrance retains its final `Единожды.`. The full edition's distinct `и нам и всем` ending is retained as a negative match for the dated book's `и нами всем`. The former whole-text-rubric baseline counts remain unchanged in their provenance; splitting these leading directions adds seven blocks on 11/12 November and six on 15 November

- `translation-prayer-boundaries-short.html` and `translation-prayer-boundaries-full.html` retain complete published originals from twenty-two source rows. Russian prayer bodies are synthetic; verified directions, speaker labels and the repaired response/prayer paragraph boundary remain. `translation-prayer-boundaries-v7.json` is the synthetic corpus extracted before the four subsequent prefix changes. The regression preserves every old key and permits only removal of an identified leading direction from the synthetic Russian value. The exact communion invitation comes from the short edition; the longer Church Slavonic direction in the full edition remains unmodified
- `translation-prayer-boundaries-provenance.json` identifies ten real paragraphs (seven visible, three altar), including nine previously false RUBRIC, across three dates and two temple variants. It records dated-source and fixture SHA-256 values, source rows, full-original reading-order hashes and the pre-change block counts. The remaining named-evangelist and temple/city text mismatches are intentional negative matches. The separate dismissal issue is unchanged
- `liturgy-2026-11-12-church10.html` is an original-only fixture cleaned from the public dated source for the accepted temple variant. The full and cleaned documents parse to equal books. Its 577 pre-change original blocks, IDs, kinds, speakers, scripture references, altar scopes and chapters were checked against the book accepted on the Android device. Full-download, cleaned-fixture and accepted-book SHA-256 values are recorded separately in the prayer-boundaries provenance; no modern parallel translation is included in this fixture

- `shared-russian-{vsenoshhnoe-bdenie,chas-tretij,panihida,oktoih7}.html` retains source-side DOM cells for fifteen exact prayer matches from four published pages. Modern Russian bodies are replaced by synthetic row/speaker markers. `shared-russian-provenance.json` records source/fixture SHA-256 values, source rows, exact originals, published Russian hashes and the four VERSION8 baseline missing-ID sets. These fixtures cover 9/7/12/7 visible additions and one altar addition per book without changing parser boundaries, names or Scripture. The real translated bodies and archive repository replay remain in local evidence only
- `translation-visible-boundaries-{full,short}.html` preserves the paired leading-span structure for two exact liturgy prayers, plus the short edition's preceding-reply and quoted-instruction negatives. Modern Russian prayer bodies are synthetic. `translation-visible-boundaries-provenance.json` records source/fixture hashes, one-based source rows, twelve visible C9 mixed blocks on four dated/temple fixtures, the original reading-order digest and every unaffected structural ID/scope/speaker digest. Seven dated-book prefixes are separate from the two strict bilingual direction pairs; altar fragments and dismissal are unchanged
